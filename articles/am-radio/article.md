# Getting Started: AM Radio
I will go over how to make a simple AM radio with minimal components and explain how each part works.

## What You'll Need

- Bread board
- AM Antenna or long wire
- Magnet Wire and rod (wood, pvc pipe, but no metal) or Inductor Kit
- Electrolytic Capacitor and Ceramic Capacitor Kit
- Resistor Kit
- Germanium Diode
- Speaker (2Ohm is good)
- 1x LM386 module
- 9V Battery
- (Optional) 3 way guitar switch
- (Optional) On/Off switch (SPST)
- (Optional) 10k logarithmic potentiometer
